FAQ

What people usually ask me

I've never trained before. Is this for me?

Yes. Starting from zero is actually an advantage — there are no badly learned habits to undo. We start with the basics: how to move, how to breathe, how to progress without getting hurt, and build from there.

I have an injury or chronic pain. Can I train?

In most cases yes, with adaptations. I'm a sports physical therapist: assessment comes first, and the plan is built around what your body tolerates today. If your case needs in-person medical care, I'll tell you plainly and point you in the right direction.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

Not necessarily. I work with what you have: a full gym, a pair of dumbbells, bands, or just your bodyweight. The plan adapts to the equipment — not the other way around.

How much time do I need per week?

You tell me, and I design around it. Three well-programmed sessions of 40–50 minutes produce real change. I'd rather give you a two-day plan you actually follow than a six-day plan you abandon in three weeks.

How does online coaching work?

Initial video consultation, plan delivery, follow-up by chat and periodic video reviews. You record your sets when technique questions come up and I correct them. It works from any country and any time zone.

How long until I see results?

It depends on your starting point, your rest and your consistency. Energy, mobility and pain usually improve in the first 3–4 weeks; strength and body composition changes consolidate from weeks 8–12. Anyone who promises you an exact date without knowing you is selling something.

What languages do you work in?

English and Spanish. You can switch this site's version with the ES / EN selector in the menu.

Do you believe you're more than a challenge?

Then you deserve real support. Write to me and let's talk with no obligation: if I'm not the right person for your case, I'll say so and point you to someone who is.
