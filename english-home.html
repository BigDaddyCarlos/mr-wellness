How we work

Four steps, zero improvising.

I won't send you a standard PDF. We build a strategy that adjusts with you.

1

Listen

Your story, your injuries, your context and what you have already tried. This is where 80 % of the plan is decided.

2

Assess

Movement, capacities and real limitations: what you can do today, not what you should be able to do.

3

Program

Your plan, with load, frequency, progression and recovery fitted to your life.

4

Adjust

Regular check-ins. If your fatigue, sleep or schedule changes, the plan changes.

About Carlos Zamora

The person who listens is the one who designs your plan.

I am a sports physical therapist and personal training specialist. I started as a trainer in 2010 and earned my personal trainer certification at Sport City University. In 2012 I started teaching combat as a sensei: jujutsu, boxing and muay boran.

Then I studied for four years: acupuncture, chiropractic and a lot more. And for the first four years after studying I gave my work away. I did not dare to charge. I spent them helping people move forward with my best will.

My work starts by listening to your story. Then come physiology and biomechanics, and in the end a plan that fits your real week.

My scope, plainly: I am not a physician or a dietitian. When your case needs someone else, I tell you plainly and point you to someone who can help.

Your body isn't an average. Your plan shouldn't be either.
